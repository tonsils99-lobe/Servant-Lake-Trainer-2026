## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Servant of the Lake**. This trainer provides optional assistance for exploring the Vanderboom house, managing puzzle interactions, tracking household tasks, and progressing through the three-day weekend adventure without changing the game's core point-and-click design.

**Target Audience:** Players who want help locating interactive objects, solving puzzles, completing household duties, revisiting story content, or reducing repetition while exploring the Vanderboom estate.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Servant of the Lake uses classic point-and-click exploration rather than direct character movement. Click room exits and interactive areas to move through the estate. The Interaction Highlight feature can reveal usable locations and exits.

### Tools & Inventory

Collect objects from rooms and scenes, then select them from the inventory before applying them to compatible targets. The Inventory Item Highlight feature helps identify useful items and possible combinations without altering the game's item-based puzzle structure.

### World & Environment

Explore the Vanderboom house room by room while completing daily servant duties, caring for the family, welcoming the visitor, and uncovering dark alchemical secrets. The trainer's task and interaction aids are intended to support exploration without replacing the game's narrative progression.

### Combat & Weapons

There is no combat gameplay in Servant of the Lake. Progress is based on exploration, item use, dialogue, environmental interaction, household tasks, and solving surreal puzzles.
